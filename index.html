Engineering Playbook

4 FinOps Rules for Lean Infrastructure

Proven architecture patterns to keep monthly infrastructure burn minimal without sacrificing reliability.

01

Right-Size Before You Scale

80% of virtual instances run at less than 15% average CPU utilization. Downgrading oversized VMs or adopting modern ARM Graviton instances immediately yields 20–40% cost reduction.

02

Automate Storage Lifecycle Policies

Transition object storage buckets from Standard to Infrequent Access (IA) or Glacier Archive after 30–90 days. Avoid paying prime storage rates on unaccessed log dumps and backups.

03

Leverage Burst Serverless for Low-Duty Workloads

If your application services background cron jobs or periodic webhooks, pay-per-millisecond serverless functions run practically free compared to keeping dedicated 24/7 VMs idle.

04

Keep Inter-Service Traffic in Same Availability Zone

Cross-AZ data transfer within AWS or GCP is secretly billed at $0.01/GB in each direction. Co-locating chatty microservices within the same AZ eliminates internal transit fees.
